          Where the temperature numbers come from
          

            Each city carries a table of monthly average temperatures. The simulation adds a
            heat-island effect on top of that baseline, calculated from how much sunlight the
            city’s surfaces absorb. Swap the table → the whole model moves to that city.
            The 8 built-in cities use approximate 1991–2020 normals typed into the file. A
            typed location fetches real reanalysis data automatically: 10 years of daily
            temperatures (ERA5, 2014–2023) from the free Open-Meteo API, averaged into the
            monthly table below at author time.
